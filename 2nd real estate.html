The Project

Architecture that belongs to its surroundings.

THE HEIGHTS rises from its site rather than imposing on it — every terrace, setback and material choice responds to sun path, wind and the skyline it joins.

180 residences across 25 floors, three typologies, and a single conviction: that a home should feel considered from the moment you enter the lobby to the last light of evening on your balcony.

Architecture

Four ideas shape every elevation.

  • 01Form

    A tapering silhouette that reduces wind load while sharpening the skyline profile.

  • 02Light

    Deep-set balconies and floor-to-ceiling glazing bring daylight into every principal room.

  • 03Material

    Board-formed concrete, bronze detailing and stone — materials chosen to age with dignity.

  • 04Space

    Generous circulation and double-height lobbies that never feel like transit space.
